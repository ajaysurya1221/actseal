## Changes

- `_read_complete_body` now requests
  `min(_READ_CHUNK, MAX_RESPONSE_BYTES + 1 - len(data))` bytes per read
  instead of a fixed 65536. Once the accepted cap (1 MiB) is reached, the next
  read asks for exactly one detection byte: an empty result proves end of
  stream (complete at cap), a byte proves over-cap. No more than cap + 1 body
  bytes are ever requested or consumed, whatever `Content-Length` or chunking
  declares. The complete-framing checks (`length` remaining -> `unavailable`
  with `jev.transport:incomplete_body`; `IncompleteRead` -> `unavailable`;
  close-delimited complete at end of stream), the oversize mapping
  (`jev.body_oversized`), close-on-every-path, one attempt and the timeout
  mapping are unchanged. Docstring of the reader states the bound.
- Module docstring: the stale "keeps it solely inside the transport's
  `Authorization` header" is corrected to say the key is kept only inside the
  private transport object, in two places: the `Authorization` header it
  sends and the value it compares successful bodies against for the
  credential-echo check. Detection scope is unchanged.
- Tests (`tests/unit/test_jev.py`, 123 collected, was 115):
  - The enlarged-budget assertion near the former line 806 is replaced. The
    scripted double now records bytes actually delivered;
    `test_oversized_body_is_malformed_after_a_bounded_read` asserts the exact
    read budgets `[65536] * 16 + [1]` and exactly cap + 1 bytes consumed for a
    cap + 1 body, a 4x-cap body and a body with a declared length of 10^9,
    and exactly cap bytes consumed (same budgets, success) for an at-cap body.
  - A `CountingResponse` pass-through over the genuine `HTTPResponse` records
    requested amounts and delivered bytes.
    `test_real_response_consumes_at_most_one_detection_byte_beyond_the_cap`
    covers fixed-length, chunked and close-delimited framing at the cap
    (success, byte-exact, delivered == cap, response closed by the detection
    read), at cap + 1 and at twice the cap (both `jev.body_oversized`,
    delivered == cap + 1, budgets `[65536] * 16 + [1]`). The existing real
    over-cap test additionally asserts delivered == cap + 1 and the exact
    budgets. No test was weakened or skipped.
