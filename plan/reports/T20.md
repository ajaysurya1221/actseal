Deviations from spec (with reason)

- `clopper_pearson` (ARCI lines 116-120) and `_validate` (lines 22-25) are
  within the approved line range but were not ported: CONTRACTS section 5
  freezes only `clopper_pearson_tail`, and assessment will call it with the
  `alpha / 4` tail directly. The golden tests convert confidence to tail with
  the identical upstream expression, so the recorded vectors are checked under
  the same semantics. Codex may ask for the two units to be added; it is a
  pure addition.
- Type-boundary rejections use `TypeError` (bool/non-integer counts, bool or
  non-numeric tail) and `ValueError` (ranges, non-finite tail). CONTRACTS says
  "reject" without naming the exception; ARCI uses `ValueError` only for ranges.
- Three tooling-only edits to copied lines (one `int` annotation, two `noqa`
  markers) are recorded in the module header as local modifications. Semantics
  and arithmetic are byte-identical to the source otherwise.
- `tests/unit/test_stats.py` uses two `# noqa: S311` markers on seeded
  `random.Random` constructors (deterministic test inputs copied from ARCI) and
  two narrow `# type: ignore[arg-type]` markers where wrong-typed inputs are
  passed on purpose. No ignores hide a failing check.
- ARCI `tests/unit/stats/test_stats.py` (reference values, monotonicity,
  symmetry, bounded coverage) was named in ADR 0002 but its text was not in the
  dispatch packet and the offline rule applies; equivalent monotonicity,
  nesting, symmetry and exact-coverage tests were written independently rather
  than copied. Codex can compare them against the upstream file.
